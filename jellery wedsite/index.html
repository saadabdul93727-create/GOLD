A Family Passion for Quality &
Craft

Fred Coates has been a collector for over 50 years, specialising in fine art, glass,
porcelain, and rare collectables. His refined eye for quality is reflected across
the collection, including the most recent addition of pewter trinket boxes.


Elizabeth Coates began jewellery making as a creative pursuit, handcrafting
pieces using Swarovski crystals, gemstones, precious metals, handmade glass
beads, and natural shells such as New Zealand Paua (Abalone). Many designs
are custom-made and individually crafted.


The collection has been presented at major Australian venues including Floriade Canberra,
the Sydney Royal Easter Show, and national craft exhibitions.
